# A Guide for English Readers

> **This page is a guide, not a translation.**
>
> The *Daodejing* 道德经 has many fine English translations. What is new here is not a translation but a **structure**. The full text, and the author's commentary, are in Chinese — this page tells you what is here and how to move around it.

## What this is

The *Daodejing* is 81 short chapters in Classical Chinese, traditionally attributed to Laozi 老子.

This project does **not** translate it and does **not** add to it. It does one thing: **restructures it.**

Every sentence of the original is kept — nothing added, nothing dropped — but the chapters and sentences are **re-ordered by meaning** into a logical whole.

## The four steps

**1 · Line-by-line reading** — 01.md–81.md
Each chapter has five sections: original text · character notes · literal rendering · commentary · related chapters.

**2 · Grouping, clarifying, evaluating** — 88.md
The 81 chapters are broken down into sentences and sorted into **4 parts / 21 groups**.

**3 · The re-edition** — 00.md
All sentences re-ordered by meaning. Every sentence keeps a marker of its source chapter, so nothing becomes untraceable.

**4 · The star map** — 天道星图 (tian-wang.html), titled on the page after chapter 73: 天网恢恢，疏而不失
An interactive map. **The Dao at the centre** — the centre *is* the group A1 (道生万物, "the Dao gives birth to the one"); three rings outward for parts 2–4, with 20 groups as nodes; two kinds of link — *source → use* (a principle flowing into its application) and *shared chapters* (two groups drawing on the same chapter). **Click any node** to read that group's original sentences, the commentary, and its connections.

## The four parts

| | Chinese | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **I** | 一、道生万物论（宇宙起源） | The Dao gives birth to all things — cosmology |
| **II** | 二、道的属性和规律（自然规律） | The attributes and patterns of the Dao — natural law |
| **III** | 三、修身成圣（尊道不争） | Cultivating oneself — following the Dao, not contending |
| **IV** | 四、治国取天下（尊道无为） | Governing — ruling by non-action |

## A note on originality

Very little knowledge is wholly original.

Most knowledge is repetition, repair, recombination, and evaluation of what already exists.

**Evaluating and restructuring existing knowledge is also creation.**

Useful knowledge should be copied and spread widely. But to copy wholesale and claim it as your own is shameful. Creators deserve real respect.
